Designed and developed a responsive fitness web experience that helps users explore training programs, generate a personalized workout recommendation, run guided workout intervals, and track weekly session progress. ## Ready-to-publish post

I’m excited to share **FitFlow Fitness**, a responsive fitness web experience I designed and developed from concept to deployment.

The goal was to go beyond a traditional landing page and create a product users can actually interact with:

- Personalized workout plan recommendations
- Filterable training programs
- Weekly workout tracker with saved progress
- Guided HIIT, strength, and mobility interval timer
- Installable PWA with offline-ready core screens
- Responsive mobile-first experience
- Accessible keyboard and reduced-motion support
- Custom social sharing metadata and branded preview

Built with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, and modern CSS**.

Live project: https://fitflow-gym-online.netlify.app

Source code: https://github.com/rahman-997/fitflow-gym
